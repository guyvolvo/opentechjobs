OpenTechJobs

Loading board statistics.

Straight from the source.

Every listing is read from the company's own hiring system, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and more, and from the careers sites of Microsoft, Google, Apple and Amazon. Nothing is reposted from another board.

Ranked against your CV.

Analyze a CV on your account page and Best matches orders the board by the skills you have. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Pay, even when it isn't posted.

A listing without a salary gets an estimate from disclosed pay for the same kind of role, level and place, and it says it is an estimate.

Alerts for a saved search.

Save any search and get an email when new listings match it.

An open API.

The job data is public and free. No key and no sign-up: poll it and build on it.

Every listing's whole life.

When it was first seen, when it was last seen, and when it closed. Explore runs SQL over all of it in your browser.
