Direct-source listings

Every listing is read from the company's own hiring system, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters and a dozen more, or from its career site, as with Microsoft, Google, Apple and Amazon. Nothing is reposted from another board, and nothing can be paid into the ranking.

CV keyword matching

Analyze a CV on your account page and Best matches orders the board by the skills you have. The file never leaves your browser. It is read where you dropped it; only the list of skills is saved, and only if you sign in.

Salary estimation

A listing with no pay posted gets a figure from disclosed salaries for the same kind of role, level and place. Every one of those is marked Est. on the row, so an estimate is never mistaken for what the company said.

Smart alerts

Save any search and get an email when new listings match it.

Build on the same data.

The whole index is a public JSON API: every listing, its skills, its salary, and the day it opened and closed. No key, no sign-up. Fair-use limits apply, 20 requests a second.
